B.Ed Overall Syllabus

Bachelor of Education is a 2-year professional programme designed to prepare skilled and reflective teachers for secondary and senior secondary levels.

Note: Syllabus may slightly vary according to university (NCTE guidelines followed).

Year 1 – Foundation

  • Childhood and Growing Up
  • Contemporary India and Education
  • Learning and Teaching
  • Language across the Curriculum
  • Understanding Disciplines and Subjects
  • Pedagogy of School Subject – Part 1
  • Reading and Reflecting on Texts
  • Drama and Art in Education

Year 2 – Advanced & Practical

  • Knowledge and Curriculum
  • Assessment for Learning
  • Creating an Inclusive School
  • Gender, School and Society
  • Pedagogy of School Subject – Part 2
  • Critical Understanding of ICT
  • School Internship (Major Practical)

Practical Components

Important Practicals
• Micro Teaching
• Simulated Teaching
• School Observation
• Lesson Planning
• Internship (16 weeks)
• Action Research
• Community Work

Educational Psychology

This subject helps future teachers understand how students learn, grow and behave in the classroom.

Major Units

  • Growth and Development (Physical, Cognitive, Social, Emotional)
  • Theories of Learning (Piaget, Vygotsky, Bruner, Skinner)
  • Intelligence and Creativity
  • Motivation and Learning
  • Individual Differences
  • Personality and Mental Health
  • Guidance and Counselling
Key Point 
Learning is relatively permanent change 
in behaviour that occurs as a result of 
experience or practice.

Three major domains of learning:
1. Cognitive Domain
2. Affective Domain
3. Psychomotor Domain

Pedagogy & Teaching Methods

Pedagogy focuses on the art and science of teaching. It covers how to plan, deliver and evaluate effective lessons.

Important Topics

  • Concept of Teaching and Learning
  • Maxims of Teaching
  • Methods of Teaching (Lecture, Discussion, Project, Problem Solving)
  • Lesson Planning (Herbartian, Bloom’s Taxonomy)
  • Micro Teaching Skills
  • Classroom Management
  • Teaching Aids and ICT
Micro Teaching Skills 
1. Skill of Introduction
2. Skill of Questioning
3. Skill of Explanation
4. Skill of Stimulus Variation
5. Skill of Reinforcement
6. Skill of Blackboard Writing
7. Skill of Closure

Curriculum & Assessment

This subject deals with designing curriculum, continuous evaluation and creating inclusive classrooms for all learners.

Key Areas

  • Meaning and Types of Curriculum
  • Curriculum Construction Process
  • Assessment for Learning vs Assessment of Learning
  • Continuous and Comprehensive Evaluation (CCE)
  • Tools and Techniques of Evaluation
  • Inclusive Education
  • Children with Special Needs

6. Introduction
7. Presentation (with Blackboard Summary)
8. Recapitulation
9. Evaluation
10. Homework / Assignment
